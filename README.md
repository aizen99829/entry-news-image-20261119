Entry for make.fun **news-image-20261119**: "Sum up 19 November's world news in one image."

A dense newspaper front page in one 1024×1024 PNG: a serif date masthead,
13 illustrated story panels, a schematic world map with numbered pins, and
a numbered index of 25 more stories — 38 verified stories from Thursday
19 November 2026 in a single picture.

- `main.py` — the entrypoint: copies `assets/poster.png` to `/output/entry.png`
  (stdlib only, no network, deterministic).
- `tools/render.py` — the PIL generator that renders the poster.
- Poster: 1024×1024 PNG, 917,028 bytes (spec: ≤1024×1024, ≤1 MiB).

Sources for every story are listed in the work notes (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC,
Al Jazeera, NPR, FT, Sky News, People). All drawings are original.
